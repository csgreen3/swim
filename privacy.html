Swimcord privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026. Applies to the Swimcord Android app, the Swimcord Garmin watch app, and the Swimcord web app.

The short version

Swimcord doesn't have a server, an account, ads or analytics. Your training data stays on your own devices. We never receive it.

What the apps handle

  • Wrist motion and heart rate are read by the watch app while you record a set, turned into stroke count, stroke rate, rhythm, sweep and an effort score, and saved in the activity on your watch.
  • Live metrics are sent from the watch to the Swimcord Android app over Bluetooth, through Garmin's own Garmin Connect app. Garmin's handling of that link is covered by Garmin's privacy policy.
  • Your sets are saved in the Android app's private storage on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Settings such as stroke style are stored on the phone.

Share live data (off by default)

If you turn on Settings › Share live data, the Android app serves your live stroke data on your local network so another app (such as OpenRide) can show it. Anything on that network can read the live stream while it's on. Turn it off when you don't need it.

Permissions the Android app asks for

  • Notifications: to show that recording is running while the phone is locked.
  • Foreground service / connected device: to keep receiving from the watch with the screen off.
  • Network: only for Share live data, on your local network.

Children

Swimcord isn't directed at children under 13.
